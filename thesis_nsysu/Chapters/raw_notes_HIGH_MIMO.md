# HIGH-ORDER MIMO DESIGN

If we detailed Parameters on Metasurface Antenna Lens, We can begin with unit cell.

**Parameters on unitcell design:**

* Dimension
* Variance of design parameters (Depend on design)
* Magnitude of unit cell, must be above -3dB for Transmitarray system
* Range of Phase of unitcell, must covering full 360 degree

If we can found out full range of Phase unitcell, then we have flexibility Metasurface Antenna Lens design on surface dimension, and Expected Radius degree.
